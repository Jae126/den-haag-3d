Design Concept

Vision & Mission

Vision
To create an interactive urban interface that bridges digital city data and the physical city, enabling people to explore and understand The Hague's urban challenges through hands-on, spatial interaction.

Mission
We translate complex municipal and geospatial data into an intuitive, interactive system using tablet-based controls and large-scale projection mapping on a 3D city model.
By allowing users to toggle, compare, and layer urban data in real time, the project supports discussion, exploration, and public engagement around mobility, climate, infrastructure, and liveability in The Hague.
